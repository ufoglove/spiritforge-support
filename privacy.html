重點
  • 《百煉靈器》（以下稱「本遊戲」）不收集、不傳送、不分享任何個人資料。
  • 沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具，也沒有使用任何第三方 SDK。
  • 遊戲本身不連網；進度只存在你的裝置上。
  • App 內購買由 Apple 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資訊。

1. 誰負責這份政策

本遊戲由 App Store 頁面上標示為「銷售者」的開發者開發與發行（以下稱「我們」）。對本政策有任何問題，請寫信到 ufoglove1@gmail.com。

2. 我們收集哪些資料

沒有。本遊戲不會把任何資料從你的裝置傳給我們或任何第三方。我們沒有伺服器接收你的遊戲資料，也看不到你的進度、裝置識別碼、位置、聯絡人或其他資訊。

3. 存在你裝置上的資料

為了讓你下次打開能接著玩，本遊戲會在你的裝置上存放以下資料。這些資料只存在你的裝置上，我們讀不到：

  • 遊戲進度與設定（例如樓層、兵器、你自己取的兵器名稱、音效開關），存放在 App 的本機儲存空間，另外在 App 的系統偏好設定（UserDefaults）裡留一份備份，避免資料遺失。
  • 是否已購買「完整版」的標記，讓沒有網路時也能正常遊玩。

如果你開啟了裝置的 iCloud 備份或電腦備份，這些資料會依 Apple 的條款包含在你的裝置備份裡，我們同樣讀不到。

4. App 內購買

「完整版」是透過 Apple 的 App 內購買機制購買，付款、帳單與收據都由 Apple 處理，適用 Apple 隱私權政策。本遊戲只會在你的裝置上向 Apple 的 StoreKit 確認你是否擁有「完整版」，不會把交易資料傳給我們。

5. 剪貼簿

只有在你按下「複製兵器碼」時，本遊戲才會把兵器碼寫進剪貼簿。本遊戲不會自動讀取你的剪貼簿；匯入兵器碼時，要由你自己把代碼貼進輸入框。

6. 當機報告與使用統計

本遊戲沒有內建任何當機回報或分析工具。如果你在裝置上同意「與 App 開發者分享」分析資料，Apple 可能會依它的條款，提供我們彙總後的使用統計與當機紀錄。這些資料由 Apple 收集、彙整，我們只用來修正錯誤、改善遊戲，你可以隨時在裝置的「設定」裡關閉。

7. 兒童

本遊戲不向任何人（包括兒童）收集個人資料。

8. 資料保存與刪除

我們沒有保存你的任何資料，所以沒有需要刪除的資料。你可以：

  • 在遊戲大廳按「設定・關於」→「重置進度」，清除遊戲進度；
  • 刪除 App，會連同所有本機資料一起刪除（刪除前請確認不再需要這份進度）。

「完整版」的購買紀錄由 Apple 保存在你的 Apple 帳號裡，重新安裝後可以用「恢復購買」取回。

9. 政策變更

如果之後的版本加入連線功能（例如線上排行榜），我們會在推出前更新這份政策與 App Store 上的隱私權標示，並修改上方的生效日期。

English

In short
  • SpiritForge ("the game") does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The game works offline. Your progress is stored only on your device.
  • In-App Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment information.

1. Who is responsible

The game is developed and published by the developer shown as the "Seller" on its App Store page ("we"). For any question about this policy, email ufoglove1@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. The game does not send any data from your device to us or to any third party. We run no server that receives game data, and we cannot see your progress, device identifiers, location, contacts or any other information.

3. Data stored on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game stores the following on your device only. We cannot access it:

  • Game progress and settings (for example floors reached, weapons, the names you give your weapons, and the sound setting). These are kept in the app's local storage, with a backup copy in the app's preferences (UserDefaults) to prevent data loss.
  • A flag recording whether you own the Full Version, so the game also works without a connection.

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, this data is included in your device backup under Apple's terms. We cannot access it there either.

4. In-App Purchases

The "Full Version" is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Payment, billing and receipts are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The game only checks with Apple's StoreKit, on your device, whether you own the Full Version. It does not send transaction data to us.

5. Clipboard

The game writes a weapon code to the clipboard only when you tap "copy weapon code". It never reads your clipboard on its own. To import a code, you paste it into a text field yourself.

6. Crash reports and usage statistics

The game contains no crash-reporting or analytics tools. If you have agreed to "Share with App Developers" on your device, Apple may give us aggregated usage statistics and crash logs under its terms. Apple collects and aggregates this data, and we use it only to fix bugs and improve the game. You can turn sharing off at any time in your device's Settings.

7. Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can:

  • tap "設定・關於" (Settings & About) → "重置進度" (Reset Progress) in the lobby to clear your progress;
  • delete the app, which removes all of its local data. Make sure you no longer need that progress first.

Apple keeps your Full Version purchase in your Apple Account. After reinstalling, use "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases) to get it back.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online features (such as online leaderboards), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
