常見問題

買過「完整版」，換手機或重新安裝後要怎麼取回？

用同一個 Apple 帳號登入 App Store，打開遊戲的購買頁（大廳的「完整版」，或點任何標著「完整版」的內容），按「恢復購買」。這不會重複扣款。

按了「購買」，畫面顯示等待核准？

如果你的帳號啟用了「購買前詢問」（家人共享的兒童帳號），購買要等家長核准。核准後遊戲會自動解鎖，不用再買一次。

家人可以一起用「完整版」嗎？

可以。「完整版」支援「家人共享」，家庭成員在自己的裝置上按「恢復購買」即可。

想要退款

App Store 的購買與退款都由 Apple 處理。請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請。退款後，「完整版」的內容會被收回，但你的遊戲進度會保留。

遊戲需要網路嗎？

不需要，遊戲可以完全離線玩。只有購買和恢復購買時要連線到 App Store。

進度會存在哪裡？會不會不見？
  • 進度只存在你的裝置上，我們沒有雲端存檔，也看不到你的進度。
  • 刪除 App 會連進度一起刪除。如果你有開 iCloud 備份或電腦備份，從備份還原裝置時，進度也會一起還原。
  • 大廳「設定・關於」裡的「重置進度」會清除進度，但不會影響「完整版」的購買。
免費版可以玩到哪裡？

萬妖塔第 1～300 層、十八層地獄第 1～6 層、序章與古劍修復前 3 份工單，以及自由鑄劍（精鋼、玄鐵兩種主材，兵器譜收 3 把）。「完整版」是一次性購買（不是訂閱），會解鎖其餘內容。

兵器碼是什麼？怎麼分享？

兵器碼（SF1. 開頭）是一串能完整重現一把劍的代碼。在兵器譜按「複製兵器碼」，貼給朋友；朋友在兵器譜的匯入欄貼上就能看到同一把劍。戰鬥不含隨機，所以同一把劍打同一個對手，結果一定相同。

FAQ

I bought the Full Version. How do I get it back on a new device or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, open the purchase screen in the game (the lobby's "完整版" button, or any content tagged "完整版"), and tap "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases). You won't be charged again.

After tapping Buy, the purchase says it is waiting for approval.

If your account uses Ask to Buy (a child account in Family Sharing), a parent must approve the purchase. The game unlocks automatically once it is approved.

Can my family share the Full Version?

Yes. The Full Version supports Family Sharing. Family members tap "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases) on their own devices.

Refunds

Apple handles App Store purchases and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. After a refund, Full Version content is locked again, but your progress is kept.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline. Only buying and restoring need a connection to the App Store.

Where is my progress saved?
  • Only on your device. There is no cloud save, and we cannot see your progress.
  • Deleting the app deletes your progress. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, restoring your device from that backup restores your progress too.
  • "重置進度" (Reset Progress), under "設定・關於" (Settings & About) in the lobby, clears your progress but does not affect your Full Version purchase.
What is free?

Demon Tower floors 1–300, Eighteen Hells layers 1–6, the prologue, the first 3 repair commissions, and the Free Forge with 2 main materials and 3 saved weapons. The Full Version is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks everything else.

Is the game available in English?

Not yet. The game is in Traditional Chinese only.
